laurelwoodestates.com. Every Wix URL fetched during the Phase 1 content
inventory is recorded here, mapped to its new path. The domain-level redirects
(westlaurelwood.com, eastlaurelwood.com, thedonastreets.com) are added in the
Phase 4 Future Work section of STATE.md and listed at the bottom here.
