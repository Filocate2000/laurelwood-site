## Intro

Laurelwood is one of Studio City's most sought-after neighborhoods, a mid-century
community born in 1958 that blends country living with city convenience. This
site is the work of Misraje Real Estate Partners, a team that does not just work
in Laurelwood, but lives here.

## West Laurelwood

West Laurelwood emerged in 1958 as part of Tract No. 24676, 290 custom homes
developed by Home Savings & Loan on land once owned by the Fryman heirs. Known
for mid-century homes, peaceful streets, and the open spaces of Fryman Canyon,
it remains one of the "Bel Air" neighborhoods of the Valley. Read its full
history.

## East Laurelwood

East Laurelwood sits on the east side of Laurel Canyon, developed through the
1960s as a hillside retreat marketed as "The Bel Air of the Valley." Custom
homes, panoramic views, and a strong community spirit define it to this day.
Read its full history.

## The Doña Streets

Laurelwood is distinguished by its Spanish-inspired street names, the Doña
streets, including Doña Emilia Dr., Doña Rosa Dr., and Doña Pegita Dr. In 2001
residents successfully petitioned to restore the correct Spanish accents on the
street signs, honoring Southern California's cultural roots. Explore the Doña
streets.
