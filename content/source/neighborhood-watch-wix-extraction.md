## SECTION: Neighborhood Watch Guidelines

Our Neighborhood Watch program is built on the idea that a safe
community is an engaged community. Below are a few key guidelines to
help ensure we all play our part in keeping the neighborhood safe:

- Be Observant: Take note of any unusual activity, such as unfamiliar
  vehicles or individuals acting suspiciously. Keep an eye on your
  neighbors' homes, especially when they are away.
- Do Not Confront: If you observe suspicious behavior, do not engage
  or confront the individuals involved. Instead, safely observe from a
  distance and report the activity to the authorities.
- Report Suspicious Activity: For non-emergency situations, contact
  Lead Officer Shawn Smith at 818-754-8358 or via email at
  33751@lapd.online. In case of emergencies, always call 911.
- Use Your Security System: Ensure your home's security cameras and
  alarms are working properly. Share any relevant footage with law
  enforcement if an incident occurs.
- Stay Connected: Keep in touch with your neighbors and be aware of
  any community updates. Regular communication helps us stay informed
  about any potential issues.

By following these simple guidelines, we can work together to maintain
a safe and secure environment for all residents.

## SECTION: Patrol Services and Neighborhood Presence

While we don't have a set patrol schedule for the neighborhood, there
are a few security services that can be seen periodically in our area.
These services add an extra layer of security, ensuring a regular
presence to deter potential criminal activity.

Even without formal patrols, you can still help keep the community
safe by:

- Being Aware: Stay alert for any unusual activity in the neighborhood
  and report it promptly to authorities or through our community
  channels.
- Home Monitoring: When neighbors are away, consider keeping an eye on
  their property, collecting packages, and notifying them of any
  unusual occurrences.
- Security Systems: Ensure your home security systems, including
  cameras and alarms, are in good working order. If you notice these
  patrol services passing by, take note of any patterns that might
  align with when homes are typically unoccupied.

While we rely on periodic professional patrols, the strength of our
community's safety also comes from individual awareness and
cooperation.
