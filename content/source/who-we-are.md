# Who We Are

Misraje Real Estate Partners is the practice of Karen and Jack Misraje, a mother-and-son partnership recognized as the #1 Two-Member Team in Coldwell Banker Global Luxury. Across more than three decades and over a billion dollars in closed transactions, they represent buyers and sellers throughout Los Angeles, with a particular attachment to the hillside streets of Laurelwood Estates above Studio City.

## A practice with its roots in Laurelwood

Laurelwood is not a place you learn from a distance. West Laurelwood, East Laurelwood, and the Doña streets each carry their own character, their own grades and outlooks and quiet, and the difference between them shows up in what a home is worth and how it should be sold. Karen and Jack have spent years on these streets rather than passing through them, and that familiarity is the foundation everything else rests on.

## Two principals, not a brand

Most clients here are handed off the moment they sign. That is not how this partnership works. Karen and Jack are two principals who divide the work and share accountability for it, so a Laurelwood seller or buyer deals with the people whose names are on the door from the first conversation through closing. In a neighborhood where owners tend to know their neighbors, that continuity is part of the reputation.

## A record that holds up in a small market

Laurelwood trades thinly. A year can pass with only a handful of sales, which means every transaction carries weight and there is little room for a misread. The partnership's track record, built over decades and across every kind of market, is what lets them advise with conviction in a setting where the comparable set is small and the stakes for getting it right are high.
